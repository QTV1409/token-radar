How the model works

The implied value of a protocol is what its token might be worth if it launched one day and the market priced it the way it prices the tokens that already trade. Nothing here is a prediction that a token will launch, or when — it is a ranking of if.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × age multiplier
  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the snapshot's tokened protocols in the same category (at least 3 of them, otherwise the global median of —). A DEX is priced like DEXes trade; a lender like lenders trade. Clamped to 0.05 – 1.50.
  • Growth multiplier — 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%) × 0.40. Fast growers score up to 1.60; shrinkers down to 0.80.
  • Age multiplier — 0.85 + 0.15 × (days since first tracked, capped at 4 years) / 4 years. A protocol that ran for years with real usage and no token scores a little higher; unknown age counts as 2 years.
